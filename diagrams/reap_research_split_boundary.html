REAP / REAP-research split — boundary and data flow

Code, tests, drivers, and cross-cutting configuration live in REAP (the public-bound package repository). Every number-bearing artifact lands in REAP-research (private, track-by-default), written by REAP's drivers through a fail-closed root locator. Embedding caches enter neither repository.
